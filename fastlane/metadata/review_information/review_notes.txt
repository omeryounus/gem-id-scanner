App Review Notes for Gem ID Scanner:

1. CAMERA & PHOTO ACCESS:
The app uses the camera to scan gemstones and minerals for real-time optical identification. You can grant camera permissions and point at any mineral, rock, or crystal to test identification. Sample photos are also available from the photo library import button.

2. IN-APP PURCHASES:
StoreKit 2 in-app purchases are enabled for Pro access. All subscription options (Monthly, Annual with 3-day trial, Lifetime) can be tested using StoreKit Sandbox / Test accounts or StoreKit configuration. Free users receive 3 complimentary scans daily.

3. OFFLINE CAPABILITY:
Stage 1 optical classification runs completely on-device without network connectivity. Stage 2 cloud verification activates when online to perform deep optical cross-referencing.
